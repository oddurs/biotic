title: The first dish
description: A seed of "tide", seventeen strains, and a culture that reached stationary phase in about twelve hundred ticks.
date: 2026-09-08
---

The first culture was seeded with the word _tide_. The mind wrote a founder that drifts along
nutrient gradients and divides when full; over the next twelve hundred ticks the mutagen
produced sixteen more strains across two generations: scroungers, hunters, a looper, a
follower. Six were alive when the dish settled into stationary phase with the agar nearly
spent.

Everything that ran is in the library as the [tide specimen](/library/tide-2026-09-08/):
the founding genome, every strain as a diff against its parent, and the growth curve with its
phases. Nothing was scored. Whatever persisted, persisted.
