## Semantic mutation

On a fraction of divisions, controlled by `BIOTIC_MUTATION_RATE`, the daughter's genome is a
rewrite of the parent's by one small heritable change: a changed threshold, a new branch on
an existing condition, a new key in memory, a dropped behaviour. The model is told that it
does not know what will work, that selection decides, and that most real mutations are bad.

## Two clocks

On the **wall clock**, `biotic live`'s default, the mutagen is a background thread and the dish
never waits on it. Requests go to a queue. If a prepared daughter exists for the strain, the
division takes it; otherwise the division is faithful and the request waits. A minimum interval
between calls, `BIOTIC_MUTAGEN_INTERVAL`, bounds the rate of novelty by how fast the mind thinks,
and the dish keeps its own time.

On the **tick clock**, `biotic run`'s default, there is no thread and no pool. The dish calls the
mind itself, from the division that rolled the mutation, at most once every
`BIOTIC_MUTAGEN_EVERY_TICKS` ticks (default 40), and waits for the reply; the daughter is born in
that division. The supply of variants is then a function of ticks and budget, not of the machine
or of `--tick`, which is what makes two flasks comparable. It costs wall time: a headless run is
as long as its calls to the model. `--clock wall|tick` picks either on either command and sticks
to the dish; `docs/experiments.md` in the repository has the precedence, the backoff in ticks and
what two flasks must share.

## The budget

Every call is priced — the endpoint's own figure when it reports one, else its price list,
cached in `vessel/prices.json` — and counted against a per-dish budget: `BIOTIC_BUDGET_USD`,
default $2.00, `--budget` on `seed`, `live` and `run`, `inf` for no cap. When it is spent the
mutagen stops and the culture grows on without variation. A failing endpoint is retried with
backoff, 15 s doubling to 10 min, and a `Retry-After` header is honoured. `biotic status` and
the vitals panel show spent / budget; `docs/budget.md` in the repository has the arithmetic.

## The mind

Any OpenAI-compatible chat endpoint. Configure it in `.env`:

```sh
OPENROUTER_API_KEY=...
BIOTIC_MODEL=qwen/qwen3-coder
```

Fully local, with no code change:

```sh
BIOTIC_BASE_URL=http://localhost:11434/v1
BIOTIC_MODEL=qwen2.5-coder:7b
```

`biotic probe` checks the wiring with one question; `biotic minds` lists available models.
