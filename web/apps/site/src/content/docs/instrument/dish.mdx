## Geometry

An elliptical grid of tiles. Everything outside the ellipse is glass: it counts as occupied
and holds nothing. Nutrient starts patchy, from a handful of gaussian blobs, and diffuses
slowly. Replenishment is nearly zero by default; `BIOTIC_REPLENISH=0` makes a truly closed
dish that blooms, crashes, and is done.

## A cell

A position, an energy level, an age, a strain id, and a memory dict that persists across
ticks and is copied into daughters. Every tick costs basal energy. Eating converts nutrient
on the tile into energy; moving and emitting pheromone cost more. Above the division
threshold a cell may split into an empty neighbour, halving its energy with the daughter.

## Death

Starvation when energy reaches zero. Senescence past a maximum age. Lysis when a genome
throws, returns something unparseable, or exceeds its wall-clock budget. Every death returns
some nutrient to the tile.

## Physics constants

All of them live in `bio/config.py`. Changing one changes every culture that runs afterwards,
which is why such changes belong in the changelog.
