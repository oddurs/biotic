## Problem

Secretion is a public good. Theory says cheaters — strains that consume
intermediates and never secrete — should invade and collapse the pipeline,
unless spatial structure or kin recognition protects producers. Whether that
happens here is the second real result the project can report, and it is a
known quantity in the literature to compare against.

## Proposal

Protocol, executed, results recorded here: 6 flasks with `compose`, `give`,
`lyse`, `hgt` on; 6 with `give`/`lyse` off (no policing possible); 30k ticks.
Measure producer share over time, pipeline throughput (end-to-end digestions
per 1,000 ticks), spatial clustering of producers (mean kin fraction of
producers vs. consumers). Report whether pipelines persist, cycle, or collapse,
and read the genomes of any strain that evolved to secrete only next to kin.
