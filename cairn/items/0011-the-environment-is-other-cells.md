Tierra's own history says where novelty comes from: not the physical
environment but the biotic one. Its parasites and hyperparasites arose because
other programs were there to be exploited. Today a biotic cell perceives its
neighbours only as occupancy and kin flags. It cannot hurt them, help them, or
borrow from them, so the only thing to adapt to is agar, and agar is simple.

This milestone gives cells three ways to matter to each other — predation,
sharing, and horizontal gene transfer — and gives the experimenter a control
arm (random, non-semantic mutation) so the project's central question can be
asked properly: does a semantic mutagen produce more ongoing novelty than a
random one, or less?

Done when: a run with all three interactions enabled has been observed for
≥ 20,000 ticks, the Tierra protocol below has been executed, and the results
are recorded in this item.
